Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Fast rats 24 h.
  2. Administer vehicle/standard/test drug daily for 1–2 days + 30 min before NSAID.
  3. Give ulcerogenic NSAID: Aspirin (500 mg/kg p.o.) or Indomethacin (20 mg/kg p.o.) suspended in 1% CMC.
  4. Withhold food/water for 4–6 h.
  5. Sacrifice by cervical decapitation.
  6. Remove stomach, open along greater curvature, wash gently.
  7. Observe under 10× microscope and score ulcers (same scale as above).
  8. (Optional) Collect & analyze gastric juice as in pylorus model.

Calculations & Scoring

Gastric analysis: • Measure pH of supernatant (1 ml diluted to 10 ml). • Titrate 1 ml supernatant vs 0.01 N NaOH: o Free acidity: To orange endpoint (Topfer’s reagent). o Total acidity: Continue to pink (phenolphthalein). o Acidity (mEq/L/100 g) = (Vol. of NaOH × Normality × 100) / 0.1. Ulcer Scoring (Intensity) • 0 = normal coloration • 0.5 = red coloration • 1 = spot ulcer • 1.5 = hemorrhagic streaks • 2 = deep ulcer (3–5 ulcers) • 3 = perforations (>5 ulcers) Ulcer Index (UI): UI=UN+US+UP10 \text{UI} = \text{UN} + \text{US} + \frac{\text{UP}}{10} UI=UN+US+10UP (where UN = avg. no. of ulcers/animal, US = avg. severity score, UP = % animals with ulcers). % Protection/Inhibition: % Protection=(UIcontrol−UItreatedUIcontrol)×100 \% \text{ Protection} = \left( \frac{\text{UI}_{\text{control}} - \text{UI}_{\text{treated}}}{\text{UI}_{\text{control}}} \right) \times 100 % Protection=(UIcontrolUIcontrol−UItreated)×100
