Aim: To Study of Anti-ulcer activity of a drug using Pylorus-ligated (SHAY) rat model and NSAIDS induced ulcer model.

Requirements / Materials

Animals

  • Wistar albino rats (150–250 g), either sex, 6 per group (CPCSEA/IAEC approval).

Standard drugs

  • Ranitidine (20–50 mg/kg p.o./i.p.) or Omeprazole (20 mg/kg p.o.).

Test drug

  • Suspended in 0.5–1% CMC (different doses).

Chemicals

  • 1% CMC (vehicle), ether/ketamine (anesthesia), 0.01 N NaOH, Topfer’s reagent (for titration), phenolphthalein.

Apparatus

  • Surgical instruments, dissecting microscope (10×), pH meter, centrifuge, burette, oral feeding tube.

Groups (minimum 3–5)

  • 1. Control (vehicle + ulcerogen).
  • 2. Standard (Ranitidine/Omeprazole + ulcerogen).
  • 3–5. Test drug (low, medium, high dose + ulcerogen).
