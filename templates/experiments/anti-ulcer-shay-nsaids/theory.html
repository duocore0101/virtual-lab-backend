Aim: To Study of Anti-ulcer activity of a drug using Pylorus-ligated (SHAY) rat model and NSAIDS induced ulcer model.

Theory/Principle

Principle

Peptic ulcers result from imbalance between aggressive factors (gastric acid, pepsin, NSAIDs) and defensive factors (mucus, bicarbonate, prostaglandins, blood flow).

  • Pylorus ligation (Shay, 1945): Ligation causes accumulation of gastric acid → autodigestion of mucosa → acute ulcers. It evaluates antisecretory activity (↓ volume, acidity).
  • NSAID-induced model: NSAIDs (aspirin 500 mg/kg or indomethacin 20 mg/kg) inhibit COX-1 → ↓ prostaglandins (PGE₂) → loss of mucosal defense → erosions/ulcers. It evaluates cytoprotective activity.

Both models are simple, reproducible, and widely used for screening H₂ blockers (Ranitidine), PPIs (Omeprazole), or herbal drugs. Ulcer index and gastric parameters quantify activity.
