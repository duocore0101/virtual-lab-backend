Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Fast rats 24–36 h (water ad libitum, prevent coprophagy with wire-bottom cages).
  2. Administer vehicle/standard/test drug (p.o. or i.p.) 30–60 min before surgery (or for 2 days in some protocols).
  3. Anesthetize with ether/ketamine. Make 1 cm midline incision below xiphoid process.
  4. Gently lift pylorus, pass thread around it, tie tight knot (avoid damaging blood vessels).
  5. Replace stomach, close abdomen with sutures. Apply collodion.
  6. Keep rats in separate cages. Allow 4–6 h (or up to 19 h in some labs) for gastric juice accumulation.
  7. Sacrifice by cervical dislocation/overdose ether.
  8. Open abdomen, tie cardiac end, remove stomach.
  9. Collect gastric contents in graduated tube (measure volume). Centrifuge (1000–2000 rpm, 10 min).
  10. Open stomach along greater curvature, wash gently with saline, observe mucosa under 10× microscope.

Calculations & Scoring

Gastric analysis: • Measure pH of supernatant (1 ml diluted to 10 ml). • Titrate 1 ml supernatant vs 0.01 N NaOH: o Free acidity: To orange endpoint (Topfer’s reagent). o Total acidity: Continue to pink (phenolphthalein). o Acidity (mEq/L/100 g) = (Vol. of NaOH × Normality × 100) / 0.1.
