Aim: To Study Bioassay of Histamine using Guinea Pig Ileum by Matching Method

Result & Conclusion

Expected Result & Interpretation

The isolated guinea pig ileum shows dose-dependent phasic contractions in response to histamine, confirming H₁ receptor-mediated smooth muscle activity.

  • Increasing doses of standard histamine produced progressively higher contraction heights.
  • The test (unknown) sample response was matched with an equi-effective standard dose.
  • The matching method proved simple, quick, and useful when test sample volume is limited.
  • Potency of the unknown sample was estimated using equi-effective dose comparison.

Conclusion

The experiment successfully demonstrates the principle of biological assay using the matching method. The graded dose-response behavior confirms receptor-mediated pharmacological action of histamine on smooth muscle.

This method is widely used in pharmacology for estimating potency of unknown samples when limited quantity is available, and for understanding dose-response relationships in isolated tissues.
