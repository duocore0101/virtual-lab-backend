📋 Procedure (Simplified Step-by-Step)

1. Sacrifice & Tissue Preparation

  • Sacrifice guinea pig by stunning (blow on head) + carotid bleeding (humane as per ethics).
  • Open abdomen, locate ileocecal junction.
  • Dissect 2–3 cm terminal ileum segment (avoid Peyer's patches).
  • Flush lumen gently with Tyrode's to remove contents.
  • Mount tissue in organ bath: One end tied to aeration tube/fixed hook, other to isotonic lever with thread.

2. Organ Bath Setup

  • Fill bath with aerated Tyrode's solution at 37°C.
  • Allow tissue to equilibrate/stabilize for 30–45 min (change solution every 10 min).
  • Adjust baseline (relaxed position) on drum.
  • Time cycle: Usually 3 min (180 sec) total — Contact time 30–60 sec + wash/recovery 2× (60 sec each).

3. Sensitivity Check & Responses

  • Add small standard histamine dose (e.g., 0.2–0.4 ml of 1 μg/ml) → record contraction.
  • Wash 2× after response peaks and returns to baseline.
  • Repeat until consistent responses (tissue sensitivity stabilizes; ileum may show initial sensitization).

4. Matching Method (Core Procedure)

  • Record responses to increasing volumes of standard histamine (S1, S2, S3...) until good range (e.g., low, medium, high contractions).
  • Typical doses: 0.1 ml, 0.2 ml, 0.4 ml, 0.6 ml, 0.8 ml of standard (1 μg/ml stock).
  • Now add test sample (T) volume (e.g., start with 0.2 ml or guess based on expected conc.).
  • Observe contraction height.
  • By trial & error:
    • If T response < nearest S → increase T volume or try higher.
    • If T response > nearest S → decrease T volume.

5. Final Steps & Recording

  • Repeat T doses until one T response closely matches (almost equal height) a standard response (S).
  • Record at least 2–3 matching pairs for accuracy (e.g., T matches S 0.4 ml).
  • Maintain fixed contact time (e.g., 60 sec), wash time, and cycle.
  • Responses plotted as height in mm on kymograph tracing.
