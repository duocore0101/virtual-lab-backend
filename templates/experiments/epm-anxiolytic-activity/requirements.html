{% load static %} Elevated Plus Maze – Requirements
Aim: To study and evaluate the anxiolytic activity of drugs using the Elevated Plus Maze (EPM) method in mice or rats.

Requirements / Materials

Materials Required

  • Animals: Healthy mice or rats (20–30 g or 150–250 g)
  • Apparatus: Elevated Plus Maze (Open/Closed arms)
  • Standard Drug: Diazepam (2 mg/kg)
  • Control: Normal saline or vehicle
  • Misc: Digital timer, observation sheet
