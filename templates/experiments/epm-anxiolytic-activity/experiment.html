Aim: To study and evaluate the anxiolytic activity of drugs using the Elevated Plus Maze (EPM) method in mice or rats.

Procedure

  1. Acclimatization: Habituate animals to the test room for 1–2 hours daily, 2–3 days prior.
  2. Grouping: Randomly divide (n=6–8): Control (vehicle), Positive control (diazepam), Test drug (3–4 doses).
  3. Drug Administration: Inject i.p. (mice: 10 mL/kg volume) or p.o. 30–60 min before the test (peak effect time).
  4. Pre-Test Handling: Gently handle animals to reduce stress.
  5. Test Execution:
    • Place animal on the central platform facing an open arm.
    • Allow free exploration for 5 minutes.
    • Record manually or via video tracking.
    • Clean the maze with 70% ethanol + water between trials to remove odors.
    • Test during the same time window (e.g., light phase).
  6. Ethical Considerations: Obtain IAEC approval; minimize stress; one-trial design (re-exposure causes one-trial tolerance).
