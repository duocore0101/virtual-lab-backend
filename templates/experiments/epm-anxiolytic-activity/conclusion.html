{% load static %} EPM Experiment – Conclusion
Aim: To study and evaluate the anxiolytic activity of drugs using the Elevated Plus Maze (EPM) method in mice or rats.

Conclusion

Result

The anxiolytic activity of the test drug was evaluated using the Elevated Plus Maze. Animals treated with anxiolytic agents (e.g., Diazepam) showed a significant increase in the number of entries and time spent in the open arms compared to the control group.

Interpretation

An increase in open arm exploration reflects a reduction in natural aversion and anxiety-related behavior. This confirms the anxiolytic properties of the drug, likely mediated via GABAA receptor modulation.

Summary

The test drug exhibited significant anxiolytic activity in the Elevated Plus Maze model. It effectively reduced avoidance behavior and enhanced exploratory tendencies in mice/rats, suggesting its potential for therapeutic use in managing anxiety disorders.
