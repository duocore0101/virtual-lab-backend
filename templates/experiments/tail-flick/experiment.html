Aim: To Study and Evaluate Analgesic Activity by Tail Flick Method
✖

Instructions

  1. Observe tail flick apparatus.
  2. Record baseline latency.
  3. Inject test drug.
  4. Record latency after drug administration.
✖

Procedure

  1. Weigh and mark the mice. Fast them overnight (12 h) with water access. Acclimatize to lab conditions.
  2. Place the mouse gently (hand-held or in loose towel) so that the distal 1–2 cm of the tail is positioned over the radiant heat source of the analgesiometer.
  3. Record the basal reaction time (0 min) – time from start of heat to tail flick/withdrawal. Select only mice with basal latency of 3–5 seconds.
  4. Administer the respective treatment (vehicle or morphine) subcutaneously.
  5. Repeat the tail flick test and record reaction time at 15, 30, 60, 90, and 120 minutes post-administration.
  6. Cut-off time = 10–12 seconds (remove tail immediately if no response to prevent injury).
  7. Clean the apparatus between animals. Take 2–3 readings per time point if needed and average.
