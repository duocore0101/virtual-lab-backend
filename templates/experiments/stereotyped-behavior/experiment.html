Step-by-Step Procedure

• Animals: Swiss albino mice (20–30 g) or Wistar rats (150–250 g), grouped (n=6 per group).

• Groups (example):

  • Group I: Control (vehicle + apomorphine).
  • Group II: Standard (chlorpromazine 3–5 mg/kg i.p. + apomorphine).
  • Group III: Test drug (various doses + apomorphine).

• Drugs and Doses:

  • Apomorphine: 1–3 mg/kg s.c. (mice) or 0.5–1 mg/kg s.c. (rats) for gnawing/climbing.
  • Pretreatment: Test/standard drug 30–60 min before apomorphine.

• Steps:

  1. Acclimatize animals to individual clear observation cages (with wire mesh floor for climbing in mice) for 30 min.
  2. Administer test/standard drug (i.p. or p.o.).
  3. After 30 min, inject apomorphine subcutaneously.
  4. Place each animal individually in the observation cage.
  5. Observe and score stereotypy immediately after injection, at intervals (e.g., every 5–10 min) for 30–60 min (peak effect at 10–20 min).
