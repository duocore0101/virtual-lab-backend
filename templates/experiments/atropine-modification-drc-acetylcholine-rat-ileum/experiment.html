📋 Experimental Procedure

  1. Mount isolated rat ileum in an organ bath and allow stabilization for 30–60 minutes.
  2. Add increasing concentrations of acetylcholine and record the contraction responses.
  3. Wash the tissue and allow full relaxation before adding the next dose.
  4. Plot the normal dose-response curve of acetylcholine.
  5. Add atropine to the organ bath and allow 5–10 minutes equilibration.
  6. Repeat acetylcholine doses in the presence of atropine and record the responses.
  7. Wash the tissue to confirm reversibility of antagonism.
