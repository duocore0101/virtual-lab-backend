📋 Procedure (Step-by-Step)

  1. Weigh the rats, number them, and acclimatize.
  2. Mark both hind paws (right and left) just beyond the tibio-tarsal junction (for consistent dipping in plethysmometer).
  3. Record initial paw volume (0 min) of both paws using plethysmometer (mercury displacement or digital).
  4. Divide into groups (n=6).
  5. Administer vehicle/standard/test drug (s.c. or p.o.) 30–60 min before carrageenan.
  6. Inject 0.1 ml of 1% carrageenan subplantarly into the left hind paw (inflamed paw). Right paw remains non-inflamed (reference).
  7. Measure paw volume of both paws at 0, 15, 30, 60, and 120 min (or up to 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 h in extended protocols).
  8. Observe animals for signs of inflammation (redness, swelling, limping).
  9. At the end, euthanize humanely as per CPCSEA guidelines.

Note: All measurements should be done by the same person for consistency. Animals fasted overnight (water ad libitum).
