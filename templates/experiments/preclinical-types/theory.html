What Are Preclinical Experiments?

Preclinical experiments are laboratory studies performed before human clinical trials (Phase I–III) to evaluate the safety and effectiveness of a drug candidate.

  • Safety (Toxicity) – Determines whether the drug causes harmful effects.
  • Efficacy (Therapeutic Effect) – Evaluates whether the drug produces the intended pharmacological action.
  • Pharmacokinetics (ADME) – Studies absorption, distribution, metabolism, and excretion of drugs.
  • Pharmacodynamics – Examines the mechanism of action and biological effects of the drug.
Main Types of Preclinical Experiments
Type Meaning (Latin) Biological System Used Complexity Level Animal Use
In Vitro "In glass" Isolated cells, enzymes, or biomolecules (test tubes, cell culture plates) Lowest None
