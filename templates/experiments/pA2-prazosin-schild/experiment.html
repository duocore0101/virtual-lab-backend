🧮 pA₂ Value Calculations

1. Calculation of Molar Concentration (Example for Sr. No. 3):

Amount: 0.1 μg/ml × 0.6 ml = 0.06 μg
Conc. in bath: 0.06 μg / 20 ml = 0.003 μg/ml
Molar conc. (M): (0.003 × 10⁻⁶ g/ml) / 383.4 ≈ 7.82 × 10⁻⁶ M

2. pA₂ Determination:

pA₂ is defined as the negative log of the molar concentration of antagonist that reduces the agonist response to 50%.

pA₂ = – log [Antagonist concentration for 50% response reduction]
pA₂: – log (7.82 × 10⁻⁶) = 6.10

📋 Procedure Step-by-Step

Step 1: Mounting & Equilibration:

  • Tie one end of muscle to tissue holder (fixed hook) and other end to lever/transducer with silk thread.
  • Mount in 20–25 mL organ bath containing aerated Krebs at 37°C.
  • Apply resting tension 0.5–1 g.
  • Allow equilibration for 30–45 min with washing every 10–15 min.

Step 2: Control Dose-Response Curve (DRC) of Noradrenaline:

  • Record cumulative DRC of NA (geometric progression, e.g., 10⁻⁹ to 10⁻⁵ M final bath concentrations).
  • Add increasing volumes of NA stock; allow contraction to plateau, record height (mm or g tension).
  • Wash thoroughly after max response.
  • Determine EC₅₀ (concentration producing 50% maximal response).

Step 3: DRC in Presence of Prazosin (usually 3 concentrations):

  • Add fixed concentration of prazosin (e.g., 10⁻⁹ M, 3×10⁻⁹ M, 10⁻⁸ M).
  • Incubate for 10–15 min (equilibration).
  • Repeat cumulative DRC of NA in presence of antagonist.
  • Wash and repeat with next higher prazosin concentration (allow full recovery between).
  • Observe parallel rightward shift with same Emax.

Step 4: Data Analysis & Schild Plot:

  • For each curve, calculate Dose Ratio (DR) = EC₅₀ (with antagonist) / EC₅₀ (control).
  • Calculate log(DR – 1).
  • Plot Schild plot: – X-axis: –log [prazosin] (molar) – Y-axis: log(DR – 1)
  • Draw best-fit straight line.
  • pA₂ = X-intercept (where log(DR–1) = 0).
  • Slope should be ≈ –1 for competitive antagonism.
