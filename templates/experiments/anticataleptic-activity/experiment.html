Aim: To study and evaluate anti-catatonic (anti-cataleptic) activity of drugs (e.g., scopolamine) against haloperidol-induced catalepsy in rats/mice.

Experiment – Anti-Catatonic Activity

Observations

Condition Cataleptic Time (sec)
Before Drug 0
After Drug —
Haloperidol induces catalepsy (sustained abnormal posture).
✖

Step-by-Step Procedure

• Animals: Albino rats (150–250 g) preferred (clearer response than mice).

• Groups (n=6):

  • Group I: Control (vehicle + inducer).
  • Group II: Standard (scopolamine 1–2 mg/kg i.p. + inducer).
  • Group III: Test drug + inducer.

• Equipment: Bar test (Horizontal bar 9–10 cm above table), Block test (Wooden blocks 3 cm and 9 cm height).

• Steps:

  1. Acclimatize animals to laboratory conditions.
  2. Administer test/standard drug 30 min before inducer.
  3. Inject inducer (haloperidol 1 mg/kg i.p. or perphenazine 5 mg/kg i.p.).
  4. Test for catalepsy at intervals (15, 30, 60, 90, 120, 150 min post-inducer).
  5. Perform tests sequentially (stage-wise).

Block Test Stages:

  • Stage 0: Place on table; note if rat moves normally.
  • Stage 1: Gently push; note if fails to move.
  • Stage 2: Place both forepaws on 3 cm block; note time to correct posture.
  • Stage 3: Place both forepaws on 9 cm block; note time to descend.

Bar Test (alternative/common):

Place both forepaws on horizontal bar; record time paws remain on bar (cutoff 180–300 sec).
