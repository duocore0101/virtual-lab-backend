{% load static %} Anti-Catatonic Activity – Requirements
Aim: To study and evaluate anti-catatonic (anti-cataleptic) activity of drugs (e.g., scopolamine) against haloperidol-induced catalepsy in rats/mice.

Requirements / Materials

Animals Used

  • Albino Rats (150–250 g)
  • OR Albino Mice (20–30 g)
  • Either sex
  • Animals are acclimatized before the experiment

Drugs Used

  • Haloperidol: (1 mg/kg, i.p.) – used to induce catalepsy
  • Standard Drug: Scopolamine or Trihexyphenidyl
  • Test Drug: (As per the protocol)
  • Control: Normal saline

Apparatus

  • Wooden or metal horizontal bar
  • Wooden blocks (3 cm and 9 cm height)
  • Stop-watch
  • Observation cage
