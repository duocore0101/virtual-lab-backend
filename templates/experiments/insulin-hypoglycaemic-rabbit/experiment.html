📋 Simulator Instructions

  1. Observe rabbit handling and blood collection technique.
  2. Record baseline blood glucose level.
  3. Inject insulin subcutaneously ($1\text{--}2$ IU/kg).
  4. Measure glucose at intervals up to $5$ hours.
✖

📋 Procedure

  1. Select healthy rabbits (usually 2–3 for the experiment; fast them for 16–18 hours with water ad libitum but no food to stabilize baseline glucose and enhance sensitivity to insulin).
  2. Weigh the rabbit and mark it.
  3. Collect initial (0 hour) blood sample from marginal ear vein (warm ear with xylene/ warm water to dilate vein; collect 0.1–0.5 mL blood).
  4. Estimate blood glucose level immediately (using glucometer or lab method).
  5. Inject insulin subcutaneously (usually in the back or flank) at a standard dose (e.g., 1–2 IU/kg body weight; prepare fresh solution if diluting).
    • Example: For a 2 kg rabbit, inject ~2–4 IU (0.05–0.1 mL of 40 IU/mL insulin).
  6. Return rabbit to cage; provide water but no food.
  7. Collect blood samples at regular intervals: 1 hr, 2 hr, 3 hr, 4 hr, and sometimes 5 hr post-injection.
  8. Measure blood glucose at each time point.
  9. (Optional: In some protocols, a control rabbit receives saline instead of insulin to show no significant change.)
  10. Observe the animal for signs of severe hypoglycaemia (convulsions, weakness); if occurs, administer glucose to rescue.
