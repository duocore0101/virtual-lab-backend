Aim: To Study Isolation and Perfusion of Frog Heart in Experimental Pharmacology

Procedure

  1. Preparation (Pithing): Double pith the frog to destroy both brain and spinal cord. Pin the frog supine on the board.
  2. Exposure: Make a midline incision, cut the sternum, and open the pericardium to expose the heart.
  3. Moistening: Keep the heart continuously moist with Frog Ringer’s solution.
  4. Cannulation: Identify the sinus venosus, insert the venous cannula, and secure with thread.
  5. Isolation: Cut the aorta, isolate the heart with the cannula, and mount it on the perfusion stand.
  6. Setup: Connect the cannula to the perfusion bottle and attach the heart apex to Starling’s heart lever.
  7. Recording: Record the normal contraction pattern on a kymograph drum.

Precautions

  • Keep the heart moist at all times.
  • Avoid air bubbles in the cannula or perfusion system.
  • Aerate the Ringer’s solution properly.
  • Maintain a constant perfusion pressure (20–30 cm).
