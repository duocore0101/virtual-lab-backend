📋 Procedure (Simplified Step-by-Step)

1. Animal Preparation & Tissue Isolation:

  • Sacrifice rat by cervical dislocation or stunning + exsanguination.
  • Open abdomen, expose uterus (Y-shaped), separate horns gently.
  • Isolate one horn (2–4 cm), flush lumen with PSS to remove contents.
  • Place in oxygenated De Jalon's/Tyrode's at room temp initially.

2. Organ Bath Setup:

  • Fill bath with aerated De Jalon's solution at 32–37°C.
  • Tie one end of horn to fixed hook/aeration tube, other end to isotonic lever with thread.
  • Adjust load (0.5–1 g) for baseline.
  • Allow equilibration 30–60 min (change solution every 10 min) until spontaneous contractions minimize.

3. Sensitivity Check & Standard DRC:

  • Add small standard oxytocin dose (e.g., 0.1–0.2 ml of 0.1 IU/ml) → record contraction (contact 30–60 sec).
  • Wash 2–3 times (60–90 sec each) → allow full relaxation.
  • Repeat until consistent responses.
  • Record graded responses to increasing standard doses (e.g., 0.1, 0.2, 0.4, 0.6, 0.8 ml of stock) — aim for 4–6 points covering low to near-maximal contraction.
  • Maintain fixed cycle: e.g., 4–5 min total (contact + wash + recovery).

4. Test Responses (Interpolation):

  • Add test (unknown) volumes (e.g., start with 0.2–0.4 ml).
  • Record contractions (select 2–3 test doses that give responses in linear range of standard curve).
  • Wash thoroughly after each.

5. Recording:

  • Measure contraction height (mm) from baseline to peak.
  • Plot standard DRC: Log dose (x-axis) vs. response height or % max (y-axis) — sigmoid curve.
