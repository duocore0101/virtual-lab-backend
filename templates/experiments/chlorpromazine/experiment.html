Procedure

  1. Place the trained mice individually inside Cooke’s pole climbing apparatus.
  2. Training involves escape from shock (unconditioned) and escape from buzzer (conditioned).
  3. Inject saline to Mice 1 (Control) and Chlorpromazine (4 mg/kg) to Mice 2.
  4. Observe if the animal climbs the pole in response to the buzzer or only after the shock.
  5. Chlorpromazine typically blocks the conditioned avoidance response.
