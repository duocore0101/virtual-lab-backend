📋 Procedure Step-by-Step

Step 1: Mounting & Equilibration:

  • Mount the ileum in 20–25 mL organ bath containing aerated Tyrode’s at 37 °C.
  • Apply resting tension 0.5–1.0 g.
  • Allow equilibration for 30–45 min with frequent washing (every 10 min).

Step 2: Cumulative Dose-Response Curve (DRC) of Histamine:

  • Add geometrically increasing concentrations of histamine (e.g., 10⁻⁹ M to 10⁻⁵ M final bath concentration or 0.01–10 µg/mL).
  • Allow each dose to produce peak contraction (plateau) before adding next higher dose (cumulative method).
  • Record height of contraction (mm or g tension).
  • After maximal response, wash thoroughly 3–4 times.
  • Repeat DRC at least once to confirm reproducibility.

Step 3: Data Analysis:

  • Measure response height for each dose.
  • Calculate % maximal response = (response height / maximal height) × 100.
  • Prepare table: Dose (µg/mL or M) | Log dose | Response (mm) | % max response.
  • Plot semi-log graph: Log concentration (X-axis) vs % maximal response (Y-axis).
  • From the graph, read the concentration corresponding to 50 % maximal response → this is EC₅₀.
  • pD₂ = –log₁₀ (EC₅₀ in molar).
