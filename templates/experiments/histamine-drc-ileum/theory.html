{% load static %} Histamine DRC Experiment – Theory
Aim: To record the cumulative dose-response curve (DRC) of histamine on isolated guinea pig ileum and to determine its pD₂ value.

Theory/Principle

Principle

pD₂ value is defined as the negative logarithm (base 10) of the molar concentration of an agonist that produces 50% of the maximal response.

pD₂ = –log EC₅₀

It is a measure of the potency of the agonist. Higher pD₂ indicates a more potent drug.

Dose Response Curve (DRC)

Guinea pig ileum contains abundant H₁ receptors which respond to histamine by producing graded isotonic contractions.

When plotted as Y-axis: % maximal response and X-axis: Log concentration, the curve becomes sigmoidal. The pD₂ value is obtained at the 50% response level from the graph.

Tissue: Isolated Guinea Pig Ileum
Receptor: Histamine (H₁)
Response: Smooth muscle contraction
