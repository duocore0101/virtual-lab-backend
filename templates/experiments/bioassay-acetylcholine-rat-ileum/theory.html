Aim: To Study Bioassay of Acetylcholine (ACh) using isolated rat ileum

Theory/Principle

Principle

Acetylcholine (ACh) produces dose-dependent contraction of the longitudinal smooth muscle of isolated rat ileum by stimulating muscarinic (M3) receptors.

The response (height of contraction) is proportional to the log dose in the linear portion of the dose-response curve.

By comparing responses of two standard doses and two test doses, the potency (concentration) of the unknown ACh solution can be calculated.

Tissue: Isolated Rat Ileum
Receptor: Muscarinic (M3)
Response: Smooth muscle contraction
