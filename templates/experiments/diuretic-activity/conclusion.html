{% load static %} Diuretic Activity Experiment – Result & Conclusion
Aim: To evaluate the diuretic activity of a given test drug by measuring urine output in rats / mice.

Result & Conclusion

Result

The average urine output in the vehicle-treated control group was 2.77 ml/rat/5 h. The Furosemide-treated group (drug in vehicle) showed 8.83 ml/rat/5 h – a 3.19-fold increase. Diuretic index = 3.19 (>1.0), confirming significant diuretic activity.

Conclusion

Furosemide (standard loop diuretic) produced a marked increase in urine volume compared to the vehicle-treated control group, demonstrating potent diuretic activity. It acts by inhibiting the Na⁺-K⁺-2Cl⁻ co-transporter in the thick ascending limb of the loop of Henle, leading to increased excretion of water, Na⁺, K⁺ and Cl⁻. The vehicle (2% CMC in saline) itself did not influence urine output, validating its use as control.
