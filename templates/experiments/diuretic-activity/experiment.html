{% load static %} Diuretic Activity Experiment
Aim: To evaluate the diuretic activity of a given test drug by measuring urine output in rats / mice.
✖

Procedure

  1. Weigh and mark the rats. Fast them overnight (12–16 h) but allow water.
  2. On the day of experiment, administer the respective treatment (vehicle or drug) orally using gavage.
  3. Immediately after dosing, hydrate all rats with 0.9% NaCl (15 ml/kg p.o.).
  4. Place rats individually or in pairs in metabolic cages. Maintain room temperature at 21 ± 0.5°C. Do not provide food or water during the experiment.
  5. Collect urine for 5 hours (record volume hourly or as total at 5 h). Take care to avoid mixing with feces.
  6. Measure the total urine volume using a graduated cylinder.
  7. (Optional advanced parameters): Measure urine pH, Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻ concentration if required by your lab.
