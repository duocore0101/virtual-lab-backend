{% load static %} Diuretic Activity Experiment – Requirements
Aim: To evaluate the diuretic activity of a given test drug by measuring urine output in rats / mice.

Requirements / Materials

  • Animals: Healthy adult male/female Albino Wistar rats (140–200 g body weight), 3–6 per group (fasted overnight with free access to water).
  • Metabolic cages (to separate urine and feces).
  • Graduated measuring cylinder.
  • Drugs/Solutions:
    • Vehicle: 2% CMC in normal saline (10 ml/kg p.o.).
    • Standard drug: Furosemide (10 mg/kg p.o., suspended in vehicle).
    • Saline load: 0.9% NaCl (15 ml/kg p.o. for hydration).
  • Other: Oral gavage needle, weighing balance, room temperature maintained at 21 ± 0.5°C.

Groups (n = 3 rats/group – standard for B.Pharm practical)

  1. Control / Vehicle-treated group: Receives vehicle (2% CMC in normal saline, 10 ml/kg p.o.).
  2. Standard / Drug-treated group: Receives Furosemide (10 mg/kg p.o. in the same vehicle) + saline load.
