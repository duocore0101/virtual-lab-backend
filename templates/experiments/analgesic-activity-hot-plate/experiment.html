Aim: To Study and Evaluate Analgesic Activity by Hot Plate Method
✖

Instructions

  1. Observe hot plate apparatus.
  2. Record reaction time before drug.
  3. Inject test drug.
  4. Record reaction time after drug.
✖

Procedure

  1. Weigh and mark the mice. Fast them overnight (12 h) with water access.
  2. Acclimatize animals to the laboratory conditions.
  3. Place each mouse individually on the hot plate (55 ± 1°C) and record the basal reaction time (0 min) – time from placement until first response (hind-paw licking or jumping, whichever appears first). Only mice showing basal latency of 5–8 seconds are selected.
  4. Administer the respective treatment (vehicle or morphine) subcutaneously.
  5. Again place each mouse on the hot plate and record reaction time at 15, 30, 60, 90, and 120 minutes post-administration.
  6. Cut-off time = 15 seconds (remove the mouse immediately if no response to avoid injury).
  7. Clean the hot plate surface between animals.
