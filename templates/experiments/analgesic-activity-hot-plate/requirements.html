Aim: To Study and Evaluate Analgesic Activity by Hot Plate Method

Requirements / Materials

  • Animals: Healthy adult Swiss albino mice (20–30 g body weight), 4–6 per group (fasted overnight, free access to water).
  • Apparatus: Eddy’s hot plate (maintained at 55 ± 1°C), stopwatch.
  • Drugs/Solutions:
    • Vehicle: Normal saline (0.9% NaCl) or 0.5–1% CMC suspension (10 ml/kg, s.c. or p.o.).
    • Standard drug: Morphine sulphate (5 mg/kg s.c., dissolved in vehicle).
    • (Optional test drug/extract suspended in same vehicle).
  • Other: Weighing balance, syringe, room temperature 22 ± 2°C.

Groups (n = 6 mice/group – typical for B.Pharm practical)

  1. Control / Vehicle-treated group: Receives vehicle only (normal saline/CMC, 10 ml/kg s.c.).
  2. Standard / Drug-treated group: Receives Morphine sulphate (5 mg/kg s.c. in vehicle).
