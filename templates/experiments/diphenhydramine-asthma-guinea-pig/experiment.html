Aim: To Study the Effect of Diphenhydramine in Experimentally Produced Asthma in Rabbit
✖

Step-Wise Procedure

To study the effect of diphenhydramine in experimentally produced asthma, always select two healthy rabbits of either sex.

Experimentally asthma is produced by using histamine aerosol apparatus. It consist of two chambers separated by perforated partition. It is provided with arrangement to spray histamine comprising of a pump, a pressure gauge, and container for histamine which is in tern connected to pump.

Inject diphenhydramine hydrochloride in a dose 2.5 mg/kg into ear vein of test rabbit. (Rabbit C)

Control rabbit (Rabbit T) receive equal volume of normal saline. After one hour place both rabbits in separate compartments and label it as Test and Control respectively. Observe normal behavior of both the rabbits.

Now histamine aerosol is introduced with the help of pump at 180 mm of Hg.

You will observe the control rabbit which has received normal saline show difficulty in breathing characterized by rapid abdominal movements, increased motor activity, state of anxiety followed by head drop.

At the same time notice the behavior of the test rabbit which has received diphenhydramine hydrochloride. It shows normal behavior.

Therefore we can say diphenhydramine, which is histamine H1 receptor antagonist has actually protected the rabbit from histamine induced difficulty in breathing.
