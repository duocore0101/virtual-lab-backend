Aim: To study and evaluate the mast cell stabilizing (anti-degranulation) activity of a given anti-allergic drug using an experimental animal model.
✖

Instructions

  1. Divide animals into 5 groups.
  2. Administer standard and test drugs.
  3. Induce mast cell degranulation using Compound 48/80.
  4. Count intact and degranulated mast cells.
  5. Calculate % Protection.
✖

Procedure

  1. Animals are divided into 5 groups (n = 6 each).
  2. Treatment is given as:
    • Group I: Normal saline
    • Group II: Degranulating agent only
    • Group III: Standard drug
    • Group IV & V: Test drug (low and high doses)
  3. After drug treatment (usually for 5–7 days), animals are sacrificed.
  4. Mesentery is carefully isolated and kept in Ringer’s solution.
  5. Tissue is exposed to degranulating agent (e.g., Compound 48/80).
  6. The mesentery is stained with toluidine blue.
  7. Slides are prepared and observed under microscope.
  8. Mast cells are counted as:
    • Intact mast cells
    • Degranulated mast cells
  9. Total 100 mast cells are counted per slide.
✖

Formula & Calculation

Formula:

% Protection = [(Degranulated cells in control − Degranulated cells in treated) / Degranulated cells in control] × 100

Example (Standard Drug):

If Control has 60 degranulated cells and Standard has 18:
(60 − 18) / 60 × 100 = 70% Protection
