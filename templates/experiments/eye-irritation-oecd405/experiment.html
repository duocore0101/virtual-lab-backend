📐 Calculation & Interpretation

Draize Scoring Components:

  • Cornea Score: (Opacity Grade × Area Grade × 5) [Max: 80]
  • Iris Score: (Iris Grade × 5) [Max: 10]
  • Conjunctiva Score: (Redness + Chemosis + Discharge) × 2 [Max: 20]
  • - Chemosis (swelling): 0 (none) to 4 (lids > half closed)
  • - Discharge: 0 (none) to 3 (severe)
  • Total Maximum Score = 110

* Record scores for treated and control eyes at each time point. Also note other effects like pannus, vascularisation, etc.

Interpretation / Classification (GHS):

  • No Category (Non-irritant): No or minimal reversible changes. Mean score < 10.
  • Category 2 (Eye Irritant): Positive response (cornea ≥ 1 or iris ≥ 1 or redness ≥ 2 or chemosis ≥ 2) in at least 2/3 animals; fully reversible within 21 days.
    • 2A: Response persists > 7 days.
    • 2B (mild): Reversible within 7 days.
  • Category 1 (Serious Eye Damage): Effects not reversible within 21 days, OR corneal opacity ≥ 3 and/or iritis > 1.5 persisting in ≥ 2 animals, OR severe persistent effects in any animal.

📋 Procedure (Step-by-Step as per OECD 405)

1. Pre-test Preparation (24 h before):

  • Examine both eyes for pre-existing abnormalities, irritation or corneal injury (use fluorescein if needed). Discard unsuitable animals.
  • Acclimatise animals to laboratory conditions.

2. Pain Management (Mandatory Refinement):

  • 60 min before: Buprenorphine 0.01 mg/kg SC (systemic analgesia).
  • 5 min before: 1–2 drops topical anaesthetic in both eyes.
  • 8 h after: Buprenorphine 0.01 mg/kg + Meloxicam 0.5 mg/kg SC.
  • Continue analgesia until lesions resolve. Untreated eye also receives anaesthetic.

3. Application of Test Substance:

  • Gently pull lower eyelid to form conjunctival sac.
  • Instil 0.1 mL (liquid) or 0.1 g (solid) into one eye.
  • Hold lids gently closed for 1 second to prevent loss.
  • Do not wash the eye for at least 24 h. Untreated eye = control.

4. Sequential Testing (to reduce animal use):

  • Initial test: 1 animal only. If serious damage observed → Terminate immediately (Category 1).
  • Confirmatory test: If no severe effects, test up to 2 additional animals.

5. Observation Period:

  • Examine eyes at: 1 h, 24 h, 48 h, 72 h (mandatory). Then daily until 21 days maximum.
  • Use fluorescein staining + slit-lamp for corneal depth.
  • Humanely euthanise if humane endpoints reached (e.g., grade 4 corneal opacity, corneal perforation).
