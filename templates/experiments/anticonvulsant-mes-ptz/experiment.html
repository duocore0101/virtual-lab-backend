Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Animal Selection and Acclimatization: Select healthy mice (18–30 g). Acclimatize to laboratory conditions for 1 hour before experiment.
  2. Grouping: Divide into groups (n=6): Control (vehicle), Standard (phenytoin), and Test drugs (low/medium/high doses).
  3. Drug Administration: Administer drug i.p. or p.o. 30–60 minutes before shock to allow peak plasma levels.
  4. Preparation for Shock: Apply electrolyte gel/saline to both corneas for good contact. Gently restrain the animal.
  5. Application of Electroshock: Deliver supramaximal shock (Mice: 50–60 mA, 0.2 sec; Rats: 150 mA, 0.2 sec). Release animal onto observation area.
  6. Observation of Seizure Phases: Observe immediately for 30–60 seconds for Tonic flexion, Tonic extension (HLTE), Clonic convulsions, and Stupor.
  7. Post-Procedure Care: Monitor recovery and provide warmth. Euthanize humanely if required per ethical guidelines.
  8. Data Recording: Note HLTE abolition and calculate % protection.

Ethical Note: Obtain IAEC approval; minimize animal distress.
