Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Animal Selection and Acclimatization: Select healthy mice. Acclimatize in observation cages for 30–60 minutes.
  2. Grouping: Divide into groups (n=6): Control (vehicle + PTZ), Standard (diazepam + PTZ), and Test drugs + PTZ.
  3. Drug Administration: Administer test/standard drug i.p. 30 minutes before PTZ.
  4. PTZ Administration: Inject 80–90 mg/kg subcutaneously in the scruff (volume ~0.1–0.2 mL).
  5. Observation: Observe continuously for 30 minutes for myoclonic jerks, onset of generalized clonic seizures, and duration.
  6. Seizure Scoring: Use Modified Racine Scale (Stage 0: No response to Stage 6: Tonic extension/death).
  7. Post-Procedure: Monitor survivors and provide necessary care.
  8. Data Recording: Note latency (sec), % protection from clonus/death, and mortality.
